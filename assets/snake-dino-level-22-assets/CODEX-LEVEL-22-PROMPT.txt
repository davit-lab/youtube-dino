COLLISION ORDER — HANDLE THIS PRECISELY
For each tick:
1. Consume one valid buffered turn.
2. Compute the next head cell.
3. Determine whether it contains food and whether the tail will move.
4. Check board bounds and body occupancy with the correct tail exception.
5. Commit movement, growth, score, and food generation once.

Moving into the current tail cell is legal when the tail vacates during that same tick. It is not legal when growth means the tail stays. Do not report a false self-collision by checking against a stale full-body occupancy set. Keep the body list and occupied-cell set synchronized. No duplicate head cells, accidental body deletion, or one-tick phantom walls.
